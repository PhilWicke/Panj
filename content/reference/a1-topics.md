# A1 topics and utterance types for Slovene (vstopna raven)

Two documents by the Center za slovenščino kot drugi in tuji jezik (CSDTJ). Printed page numbers; URLs in README.md.

- **SDTJ 2020**: *Slovenščina kot drugi in tuji jezik. Izobraževalni program za odrasle*, Ljubljana, March 2020.
- **PR 2016**: *Preživetvena raven za slovenščino, za potrebe programa Opismenjevanje v slovenščini za odrasle govorce drugih jezikov*, ed. Nataša Pirih Svetina, Ljubljana, May 2016. It updates *Preživetvena raven v slovenščini* (TiPS project, 2004) and cuts that version's 14 topics to 10 (pp. 2, 4).

## What A1 means in these documents

SDTJ 2020 calls A1 the *vstopna raven*: very simple exchanges about concrete needs (*jaz-tukaj-zdaj*), supported by gestures and a helpful partner (*naklonjen sogovorec*), with the weight on understanding and spoken interaction (pp. 12-14). Can-dos include talking about family, origin, job and hobbies, ordering food, buying a ticket, using numbers, prices and times, filling in a form, writing an SMS (pp. 13-14). Connectors stay at *in*, *potem* (p. 22). Literate beginners need about 120 to 180 course hours (p. 24).

PR 2016 treats survival-level speech as mostly formulaic, built from memorised chunks (*formule*, *vzorci*), and so has no grammar chapter (pp. 4, 6).

## Communicative functions with model utterances (PR 2016)

Each chapter lists can-do functions (*Govorci ZNAJO / SO SPOSOBNI ...*) under *govorjenje in poslušanje*, *branje* and *pisanje*, each with *vzorci*, then *besedišče* and *sociokulturno vedenje* (p. 5). Quoted examples:

- *Pozdraviti in posloviti se*: Dober dan. Živjo. Na svidenje. (p. 17)
- *Predstaviti sebe in druge osebe*, respond: Jaz sem Martin, to pa je Ana. Me veseli. (p. 17)
- Give and ask personal details: Sem iz Kanade. Stara sem devetindvajset let. Kako ti je ime? Od kod ste? (pp. 7-8)
- *Povabiti*, *sprejeti ali zavrniti*: A greva na kavo? Ja, prosim. Ne hvala. (p. 17)
- *Opravičiti se*, *nazdraviti*, *voščiti*: Oprostite. Na zdravje. Vse najboljše. (p. 17)
- Repair a conversation: Oprostite, ne razumem. A lahko ponovite? Bolj počasi, prosim. Kako se reče … po slovensko? (pp. 17-18)
- Ask the way, buy a ticket: Oprostite, kje je banka? Eno karto za Koper, prosim. Koliko stane povratna vozovnica? (p. 15)
- Shop, complain, report a loss: Kilo banan, prosim. Koliko stane? Ta majica je prevelika. Izgubil sem denarnico. (p. 25)
- Order and pay: Kavo z mlekom, prosim. Kozarec vode, prosim. Račun, prosim. (p. 27)
- Say how you feel, ask for help: Slabo se počutim. Glava me boli. Kje je lekarna? (p. 20)
- Workplace requests: Potrebujem 20 kopij. A lahko dobim en dan dopusta? (p. 29)

Reading and writing stay minimal: recognise signs and form fields, read timetables and short notes, write personal data into a form, an SMS, a postcard or a greeting (pp. 5, 8, 13, 18). Cultural notes include *vikanje* versus *tikanje* (p. 9).

## Task and text types in the A1 exam (SDTJ 2020, p. 28)

- *Branje* (25 min): branje za orientacijo, branje za iskanje podatkov in utemeljitev, branje korespondence.
- *Poslušanje* (15 min): razumevanje pogovora med drugimi govorci, poslušanje avdiomedijev in posnetkov, poslušanje obvestil in navodil.
- *Pisanje* (20 min): izpolnjevanje obrazcev, dopisovanje.
- *Govorjenje* (15 min plus 15 min preparation): družabni pogovor, monolog (opisovanje), izmenjava informacij, pridobivanje dobrin in storitev.

Pass mark: 60 % on every part (p. 29).
